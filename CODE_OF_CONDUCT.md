# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and do not harass anyone.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/antonygiomarxdev). The maintainer reviews every report and may remove content or block accounts that break these rules.
